The Data Systems Lab at POSTECH ([https://dslab.postech.ac.kr](https://dslab.postech.ac.kr)), directed by Prof. Wook-Shin Han, invites applications for a Postdoctoral Researcher position.

The lab works at the intersection of data systems and AI, and recently published at SIGMOD, VLDB, NeurIPS, ICML, and ACL.

## Research topics

The successful candidate will lead research in one or more of the following areas:

- Semantic operators and AI-native query processing
- Vector databases and multi-modal indexing
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and agentic systems
- LLM serving and inference optimization

## Qualifications

- Ph.D. in Computer Science or a closely related field (completed or near completion)
- A strong publication record at top-tier venues such as SIGMOD, VLDB, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, or ACL is required
- Solid systems-building skills and a track record of implementing research prototypes
- Ability to mentor graduate students and lead collaborative projects
- Excellent written and spoken English
